# Evidence — What the App Does (Plain-Language Requirements)

This document describes everything in the app in plain English.
**To request changes: edit anything below — cross things out, rewrite wording,
add options, reorder lists — and send it back.** The app will be updated to
match.

## 1. Home screen

A big yellow button: **I NEED INTENSITY**

Below it, five smaller buttons:

- SHOW ME EVIDENCE I'M CHANGING

- I AM THANKFUL FOR

Notice. Choose one next step. Collect evidence.*

## 3. The pause screen

After the check-in, the app says:

> You do not need to understand this yet.
> You only need to pause.
> Try one thing for 10 minutes.

Then ONE big yellow **START HERE** button showing the category the app thinks
fits best (based on what was selected), with a "Show all categories" link

Each category below shows: when the app recommends it, what it suggests

### Stimulation

### Relief

### Anger

### Arousal
- **Recommended when:** erotic energy, fantasy, body buzz

- **Reminder shown:** Arousal is allowed.

### Creativity
- **Recommended when:** urge to get high and write, idea spirals,
  breakthrough hunger

  paragraph · bad writing allowed

### Connection
- **Recommended when:** lonely, people just left, attachment ache
- **Try:** text a friend · send a thinking-of-you message · express
  appreciation · call someone · call Anthony · pet Sunny

### Gratitude
- **Recommended when:** scarcity, fear, obsession, spiraling

### Memory & Reality Check
- **Recommended when:** hopeless, stuck, "nothing is changing"

## 10. Big Decision Mode

For tattoos, purchases, relationships, job changes, quitting, starting.

1. "What is the decision?" (write it down)
2. "Have I already decided?" — yes / maybe / no
   - **Yes** → process it: What does this move me toward? What does it help
     me avoid? Who will this affect? What would waiting 24 hours cost?
   - **Maybe** → explore it: What am I hoping this changes? What need is
     underneath? What's the smallest version I could try first? Whose voice
     do I trust on this?
   - **No** → evaluate it: What are the real options, including "not yet"?
     What would future me thank me for? What evidence would help me decide?
     What happens if I wait a week?
3. Always shows: **"Am I protecting my autonomy? Or protecting access to a
   behavior?"**
4. Buttons: "Delay this decision for now" (counts as evidence) · "Save and
   step away" · "I'm activated — regulate first" (goes to the check-in)

---

## 11. Rules the app always follows

1. **No shame.** Never "you failed / you relapsed." Always "you noticed /
   you paused / you came back / evidence counts."
2. **Regulation before analysis.** No insight required to use the app.
3. **Permission is not action.**
4. **Evidence over perfection.** No streaks.
5. **Privacy:** everything logged stays on the phone it was logged on.
   Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

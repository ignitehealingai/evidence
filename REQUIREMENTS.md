# Evidence — What the App Does (Plain-Language Requirements, v3)

This document describes everything in the app in plain English.
**To request changes: edit anything below — cross things out, rewrite wording,
add options, reorder lists — and send it back.** The app will be updated to
match.

## 1. Home screen

A big green button: **I NEED INTENSITY**

Below it, five smaller buttons:

- SHOW ME EVIDENCE I'M CHANGING
- LOG A WIN
- GRATITUDE
- SUPPORT
- BIG DECISIONS

If a check-in was started but not finished, a banner appears at the top:
"You have an unfinished check-in — pick up where you left off — nothing was
lost."

At the bottom, the core promise: *You do not have to decide right now. Pause.
Notice. Choose one next step. Collect evidence.*

## 6. Evidence dashboard ("Proof I'm Changing")

Shows: total pieces of evidence, number of check-ins, counts in each
category, your most common dysregulators, and the most recent entries.
**Tapping a category opens it to show the entries behind the number.**
Every entry can be edited or deleted.

Categories counted: Pauses · Gratitudes · Support reach-outs · Cravings
survived · Honest moments · Parenting wins · Recovery wins · Boundaries
held · Difficult conversations · Meetings attended · Acts of repair ·
Sober creative sessions · Decisions delayed · Other evidence

No streaks, ever. Evidence over perfection.

At the bottom: a link to Reports, and "Erase all data on this device"
(for clearing test entries).

---

## 7. Reports

- **Report builder:** "After I [event], how often did I [event] within
  [time window]?" — combining wins, gratitudes, check-ins, reach-outs,
  pauses, and delayed decisions.
- **What follows each path?** How often a **win** (wins only — gratitudes
  are not the measure of success) followed within a day of check-ins where
  you rode it out vs. acted on it. Never framed as a scoreboard.
- **My most helpful actions:** your top 10 specific actions (not
  categories), ranked by how often the craving passed or eased after
  trying them.
- **When do cravings hit?** By time of day and day of week.
- **What shows up before I cave?** Dysregulators and feelings from
  check-ins that ended with "I acted on it."
- **What do I reach for?** Most common urges.

## 9. Log a Win

A box to write any win, with the same one-tap example list as the
reflection screen, and a category picker. Any size counts. What you type
is saved as you go — a half-written win survives closing the app.

---

## 10. Gratitude

A quick page: type anything you're thankful for and save it — as many as
you want in a row. Each is saved as evidence with date and time, and the
most recent ten are shown.

## 11. Big Decisions

For tattoos, purchases, relationships, job changes, quitting, starting.

1. "What is the decision?" (write it down)
2. "Have I already decided?" — yes / maybe / no
   - **Yes** → process it: What does this move me toward? What does it help
     me avoid? Who will this affect? What would waiting 24 hours cost?
   - **Maybe** → explore it: What am I hoping this changes? What need is
     underneath? What's the smallest version I could try first? Whose voice
     do I trust on this?
   - **No** → evaluate it: What are the real options, including "not yet"?
     What would future me thank me for? What evidence would help me decide?
     What happens if I wait a week?
3. Always shows: **"Am I protecting my autonomy? Or protecting access to a
   behavior?"**
4. Buttons: "Delay this decision for now" (counts as evidence) · "Save and
   step away" · "I'm activated — regulate first" (goes to the check-in)

---

## 12. Rules the app always follows

1. **No shame.** Never "you failed / you relapsed." Always "you noticed /
   you paused / you came back / evidence counts."
2. **Regulation before analysis.** No insight required to use the app.
3. **Permission is not action.**
4. **Evidence over perfection.** No streaks.
5. **Nothing gets lost.** Every screen saves as you go.
6. **Privacy:** everything logged stays on the phone it was logged on.
   Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
